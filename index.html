NVO987Association

The association implements cultural, artistic, educational and research projects in the fields of contemporary art, visual culture and cultural studies. It organizes exhibitions, art projects, workshops, training programs, conferences and professional programs. In collaboration with artists, researchers, educators and cultural institutions, it supports the creation of new works, research and educational initiatives. The cultural and artistic education of children and young people is a priority area of its work. Its programs include artistic activities, creative workshops, educational projects and community initiatives. Through the tools of contemporary art and visual culture, these develop creative and critical thinking, personal expression and the ability to cooperate. The association also implements specific programs aimed at reducing cultural and social inequalities. It provides disadvantaged children and young people with opportunities to access culture and education, and supports initiatives addressing the consequences of child poverty. Children's rights occupy an important place in its work. Through artistic, educational and community programs, the association contributes to raising awareness of children's rights and promoting their respect, paying particular attention to taking children's opinions into account and to their participation. In this field, it also cooperates with international organizations and professional partners. The association also conducts research projects. In addition to contemporary art and culture, it examines issues related to the visual environment, education, social transformations and cultural participation. It makes the results of its research accessible through professional events, art projects and publications. It develops national and international cooperation with artists, researchers, educators, cultural and educational institutions, associations, foundations, as well as national and international organizations. These forms of cooperation include joint projects, programs, research and professional initiatives. The association also uses and develops digital technologies for cultural, artistic and educational purposes. It creates websites, software and online applications, as well as digital content that it produces and distributes. As part of its activities, it publishes cultural and professional publications, books, journals and other publications. The association operates as a nonprofit organization and implements its programs in the service of culture, education, research, and the social and cultural equality of opportunity of children.
